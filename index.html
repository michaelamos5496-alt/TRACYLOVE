My Tracy,

There are things I feel for you that are too big for a text message and too quiet for a phone call. So I built you somewhere to keep them.

I love the woman you are. I love her on her best days, and I love her on the heavy ones.

What I see when I look at you

I am so proud of you.

Leadership training is asking a great deal of you: your body, your mind, your patience. And you keep showing up, on the days it comes easily and on the days it doesn’t. That is not a small thing, Mama. That is who you are.

For the days you need me

Keep these close.

You don’t have to be strong for me. Put it down for a minute. You are allowed to be tired, to be frustrated, to not be okay today. I think no less of you for it. Rest, my love.

Pressure has a way of making everything feel urgent and lonely at once. You are not alone in it, even when it feels that way. Your worth was never tied to what you have, or to how smoothly a hard season is going. Bring it to me. I’m here.

Look at where you are and what you are doing. On the days you can’t see it, borrow my eyes: I see a woman who is capable, disciplined, and far braver than she gives herself credit for.

I love you. Not less on your hard days. Not less on any day. You, exactly as you are.
